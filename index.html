About Kamatlux Lighting Pvt. Ltd.

Luxury Chandelier Manufacturer & Decorative Lighting Solutions in Delhi NCR

Kamatlux Lighting Pvt. Ltd. is a luxury chandelier manufacturer and decorative lighting company based in Delhi NCR, India. We offer elegant crystal chandeliers, designer jhumars, modern LED chandeliers, pendant lights and customised decorative lighting solutions for residential and commercial interiors.

With over 7 years of industry experience, we combine creative designs, quality craftsmanship and contemporary lighting concepts to enhance the beauty of every interior. From luxurious living rooms and duplex staircases to hotels, restaurants, banquet halls and commercial spaces, our lighting solutions are designed to make a lasting impression.

Our decorative lighting collection is available to customers across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad. We help customers explore chandelier and lighting options suited to homes, villas, apartments, hospitality venues and commercial interiors throughout Delhi NCR.

Whether you are looking for a statement crystal chandelier, a modern LED chandelier or a customized lighting design, Kamatlux Lighting offers solutions to complement your space and interior preferences.

Our Story

Bringing Elegance to Everyday Spaces

Lighting does more than illuminate a room. The right chandelier or decorative light can become the centrepiece of an interior, complement architectural details and influence the atmosphere of a space.

At Kamatlux Lighting, we focus on the decorative and visual role of lighting in modern interiors. Our product categories cover a range of styles, from traditional jhumars and crystal chandeliers to contemporary pendant lights, wall sconces and modern decorative fixtures.

We understand that different spaces require different lighting approaches. A grand chandelier in a double-height living room has a different purpose from a pendant light above a dining table or a wall light beside a bed.

That is why we encourage customers to consider their ceiling height, room dimensions, interior style, preferred finish and lighting requirements when selecting a product.

Based in Delhi NCR, Kamatlux Lighting serves customers looking for decorative lighting options for residential and commercial interiors.
